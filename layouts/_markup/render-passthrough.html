{{- /* Renders the math that markup.goldmark.extensions.passthrough hands over, at build time, with the KaTeX that is compiled into Hugo. `output: mathml` is deliberate: MathML is drawn by the browser itself, so a formula needs no stylesheet and no script — nothing to load, nothing to go stale, and it stays legible in both themes because it inherits the page's own text colour. (KaTeX's HTML output would look identical but drags in katex.min.css to do it.) A formula that does not parse must not take the build down with it, so errors are reported as a warning and the source is left on the page as written. */ -}} {{- $opts := dict "output" "mathml" "displayMode" (eq .Type "block") -}} {{- with try (transform.ToMath .Inner $opts) -}} {{- with .Err -}} {{- warnf "math: %s could not be rendered (%s)" $.Inner . -}} {{- $.Inner -}} {{- else -}} {{- .Value -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
