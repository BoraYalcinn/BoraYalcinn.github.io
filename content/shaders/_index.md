title: "Shaders"
description: "Fragment shaders written on Shadertoy: raymarching, noise, and assorted experiments."
---

Shaders are where my two favourite things meet. I love visual effects and the
look of a thing, and I love mathematics even more, and a fragment shader is
nothing but mathematics that you can see. Every pixel gets the same handful of
formulas, and a distance function or a noise term you can write on one line
turns into fog, or water, or a shape that was never modelled by anyone.

So I write them here for fun, and to sharpen my GLSL and HLSL along the way.
Some of these are small experiments, some are attempts at an effect I saw
somewhere and wanted to understand by rebuilding it.

Each page shows a recorded loop of the shader running, with a link through to
the Shadertoy page where you can read the source and change it yourself.
