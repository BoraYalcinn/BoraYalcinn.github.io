## About me

I am an undergraduate Computer Science student at Yeditepe University in
Istanbul, currently on a scholarship Erasmus+ exchange at UTBM in France.

What I actually work on is computer graphics: ray and path tracing, GPU
architecture, parallel computing, C++, and, most of all, the mathematics
underneath all of it. Rendering is the part of engineering where the maths stops
being abstract and turns into something you can look at, which is exactly why I
like it. Game development is the other half of that interest, and both of them
keep sending me back to the same place: linear algebra, geometry, probability.

Some of that work happens in other people's code. I contribute to open source
game engines on rendering topics, which is a different kind of exercise from
writing your own: you are reading a renderer somebody else designed, at a scale
you would never build alone, and you have to understand it well enough to change
one thing without breaking the rest.

That interest does not stop at the screen. I shoot photography, and it turns out
to be the same subject from the other side. Depth of field, exposure, how light
falls on a surface and what a lens does to it are things I model in code during
the week and chase with a camera at the weekend.

Alongside the engineering degree I am doing a minor in Business Administration.
Writing good code is not the only thing that decides whether a project gets
finished, so I would rather build the teamwork and leadership side of it
deliberately than hope I pick it up along the way.

You will find my projects here, with longer write-ups for the ones worth
explaining, and the video showcases on my YouTube channel. The links to
everything, including LinkedIn, are at the top of the page.
